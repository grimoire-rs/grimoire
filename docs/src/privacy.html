privacy

What this site processes.

Almost nothing. grimoire.rs and index.grimoire.rs are both sets of static files. There is no account, no form, no analytics, no cookie, and nothing on any page of either is loaded from another company's server. What remains is the request your browser makes to fetch the page, which the host logs — this notice exists to say so precisely, and it covers both sites.

Who is responsible

Michael Herwig — contact@michael-herwig.de. This is a private, non-commercial open-source project.

Server logs

Both sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's own documentation states that "when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed into GitHub or not." Alongside the IP address, a request of this kind ordinarily records the time, the file requested, the referring page, and your browser's user-agent string.

No cookies, no tracking

Neither site sets a cookie, and neither runs analytics, a tag manager, or any advertising or tracking script. Nothing is fetched from a third-party server while you read: the typefaces are the ones already on your system, the terminal player on the front page is served from this domain rather than a CDN, and the package logos on the index are stored in the index repository rather than hot-linked from a registry.

Both sites remember the display choices you make, in your browser's local storage: mdbook-theme and mdbook-sidebar on the documentation chapters, and theme on the package index. They stay on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and hold no identifier. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Links to other sites

Pages here link out to GitHub, npm, the Visual Studio Marketplace and container registries. Following such a link hands you to that operator, whose own privacy notice then applies. Nothing is sent to them until you click.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your personal data (Art. 15), its rectification (Art. 16) or erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Write to the address above.

One honest limitation: the log data described here is held by GitHub, and I never see it. A request about those logs is one only GitHub can answer — I will point you at them rather than pretend otherwise.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in the member state of your residence, place of work, or of the alleged infringement.
